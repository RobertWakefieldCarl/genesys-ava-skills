ava-compliance-checker

Skill overview — Genesys Cloud AVA compliance assessment from scorecard.json

Skill v1.8.0 Compendium v1.8

Not legal or compliance advice. Genesys does not provide legal advice. Assessment reports map evaluation results to a research compendium for discussion only. Customers must engage qualified counsel to confirm applicability.

What it does

ava-compliance-checker turns a published Agentic Virtual Agent (AVA) evaluation artifact — scorecard.json from ava-evaluate — into a customer-ready compliance assessment (HTML and optional PDF). It does not replace ava-corner-cases (authors security scenarios) or ava-evaluate (runs them).

  1. Loads scenario pass/fail text from the scorecard.
  2. Matches keywords to control themes in data/evidence-map.json.
  3. Cites only the bundled compendium (AI and Virtual Agent Laws, Court Cases and Compliance Standards, v1.7).
  4. Writes artifacts under axl-sessions/AXL-<customer>/compliance/<run-id>/ (scorecard snapshot, MD copy, compliance-run.json, reports).

What it covers

US federal, state, and local law; EU/UK/worldwide; AI SMS/email (§1.11); court cases (§§2.12–2.17); organizational standards (PCI, HIPAA, FedRAMP, OWASP §3.12, ADA §3.13, messaging §3.14, IRS 1075, NAIC, GLBA/NYDFS).

Default workflow

  1. Assess only — generate report; stop unless the user opts into mitigation.
  2. Optional mitigation — ava-design / ava-build; log fixes in mitigations-applied.json.
  3. Retest — ava-evaluate again; verification or mitigation-outcome report with baseline comparison.

Resolve market vertical (what business the AVA serves) before final legal readouts when inference is weak.

Example commands

# Financial services AVA
node scripts/generate-compliance-report.js ^
  --scorecard ".ava-lifecycle\my-ava\eval-runs\...\scorecard.json" ^
  --session-dir "axl-sessions/AXL-acme-bank" ^
  --financial

# P&C insurance
node scripts/generate-compliance-report.js ^
  --scorecard "...\scorecard.json" ^
  --market-vertical insurance-pc

# Domain brokerage (corner security)
node scripts/generate-compliance-report.js ^
  --scorecard "...\scorecard.json" ^
  --market-vertical domain-brokerage ^
  --evaluation-suite corner-security
